Here is my report on my final CSCI 103 project! 

## DESCRIPTION
My project is called "Judy's Light-Up FLower Power Purse." It's a hand-sewed felt envelope style clutch. It has both felt flowers and embroidered flowers. When the purse is closed, and the light sensor on the purse body detects darkness, the buzzer inside the purse sings "Ode to Joy" and the LEDs on the felt flowers light up in time to the notes of the melody. When the light sensor detects brightness, it will stop the song after one run-through. 

## MOTIVATION
I love Spring, and I love purses, so I thought I could make a Spring-inspired purse that would make the weather outside nicer and would be a good accessory.

## MATERIALS LIST
- 1 LilyPad Arduino
- 3 LilyPad LEDs (Pink)
- 1 LilyPad Light Sensor
- 1 LilyPad Buzzer
- Conductive Thread
- 1 Metal Snap
- 2 pieces 8” x 11” felt (pink, white)
- Embroidery Thread (Pink, Hot Pink)
- Thread, Scissors, Needle

## TIPS TO MY PAST SELF
1. Make sure to really think through the 3D circuitry. Sometimes even with the paper prototype, I would still get confused about which side of the fabric something was supposed to go on.
2. Make sure you understand the role that having the snap closed versus open will have on the connection of all of the negative traces. I had to really think this thorough (as you can see in my diagram above).
3. I think if I was to do this project again, I would figure out some way to hide the light sensor more. By the time I thought about maybe putting it so it would get light when the purse was open, I was too late in sewing to really figure that out.
