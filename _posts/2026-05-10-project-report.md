Here is my report on my final CSCI 103 project! 

## DESCRIPTION
My project is called "Judy's Light-Up FLower Power Purse." It's a hand-sewed felt envelope style clutch. It has both felt flowers and embroidered flowers. When the purse is closed, and the light sensor on the purse body detects darkness, the buzzer inside the purse sings "Ode to Joy" and the LEDs on the felt flowers light up in time to the notes of the melody. When the light sensor detects brightness, it will stop the song after one run-through. 

## MOTIVATION
I love Spring, and I love purses, so I thought I could make a Spring-inspired purse that would make the weather outside nicer and would be a good accessory.

## MATERIALS LIST
- 1 LilyPad Arduino
- 3 LilyPad LEDs (Pink)
- 1 LilyPad Light Sensor
- 1 LilyPad Buzzer
- Conductive Thread
- 1 Metal Snap
- 2 pieces 8” x 11” felt (pink, white)
- Embroidery Thread (Pink, Hot Pink)
- Thread, Scissors, Needle
