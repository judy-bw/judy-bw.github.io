## This is a post to show you my Arrays assignment on my Arduino.

For this assignment, we had to make a program that would look at all of the PWM pins on the arduino, randomly choose one, and assign a random brightness to it if it was off, or turn it off if it was already on.

Here's a photo (sorry about the quality, the lights happened to be quite bright) of my Arduino!
