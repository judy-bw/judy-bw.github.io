## This is a post to show you my Separate Sparklies assignment on my Arduino!

For this assignment, we had to take the worksheet from our last assignment and turn it into real code in three different arduino files. 

First, I made a file called flashbulb. Here, we wanted the arduino LED to flash on every four seconds, for one second.
Here's a photo:
![arduino-1](/assets/img/IMG_0604.JPG)

Next, I made a file called big dipper. For this LED, the LED flashes every 3 seconds, starting at second 0, and growing linearly, increasing the light by 30 every 0.1 seconds until max brightness. 
Here's a photo:
![arduino-2](/assets/img/IMG_0603.JPG)

Last, I made a file called synchronous. Here, the LED is supposed to flash on three times every 10 seconds, for 0.2 seconds each time.
Here's a photo:
![arduino-2](/assets/img/IMG_0605.JPG)


A tip I would give myself is to make sure to look at the example code posted on GLOW earlier.
